\chapter{Interest-rate and curve risk}\label{ch:risk}
\label{sec:zero-price}
Return to contractual payments. Actual/365 times \(t_i\) count settlement-to-payment calendar days, including leap days, divided by 365. Zero-discounted present values \(V_i\) and dirty currency price \(P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r]\) are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:zero-price}
t_i=\frac{\operatorname{days}(d_i-d_s)}{365},\qquad
V_i=CF_i e^{-r(t_i)t_i},\qquad P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r]=\sum_iV_i.
\end{equation}
This uses continuous compounding and the assigned zero interpretation (\cref{sec:zero-par}), separately from coupon-based \(T_i\) and YTM pricing.

\label{sec:parallel}
An additive decimal shock \(s(T)\) gives shocked rate \(r_s(T)=r(T)+s(T)\). Full repricing recalculates discounted payments; scenario profit and loss (P\&L) is currency change \(\Delta P_s=P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r+s]-P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r]\). For constant parallel amplitude \(\delta r\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:parallel-sign}
\frac{dP_{\mathrm{zero}}[r+\delta r]}{d\delta r}
=-\sum_i t_iV_i e^{-\delta r t_i}<0.
\end{equation}
Positive redemption ensures higher rates lower price.

\label{sec:shapes}
Steepening increases the long-minus-short maturity rate difference; flattening decreases it. The specified steepener assigns \((-10,0,+10)\,\bp\) at \((2,10,30)\) years; the flattener reverses these shifts. Piecewise-linear interpolation joins neighbouring anchors by straight lines, holding endpoint shifts constant outside them. These definitions are illustrative, not universal.

\section{Key-rate decomposition}\label{sec:keys}
Exposure varies by payment horizon \parencite{ho1992}. Choose \(J\) ordered key maturities \(k_j\) in years, indexed by \(j=1,\ldots,J\). Dimensionless basis weight \(b_j(T)\) equals one at its own key and zero at others, interpolating linearly with constant endpoint tails. Neighbouring weights sum to one, a partition of unity:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:partition}
b_j(k_j)=1,\qquad \sum_{j=1}^J b_j(T)=1,\qquad 0\leq b_j(T)\leq1.
\end{equation}
\label{sec:key-dv01}
Key-rate DV01 measures currency sensitivity to \(hb_j\); parallel DV01 uses constant \(h\):
\begin{align}
\mathrm{KRDV01}_j&=\frac{P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r-hb_j]-P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r+hb_j]}2,\label{eq:key-dv01}\\
\mathrm{DV01}_{\parallel}&=\frac{P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r-h]-P_{\mathrm{zero}}[r+h]}2.\label{eq:parallel-dv01}
\end{align}
\label{sec:reconciliation}
Partition makes first-order exposures additive. Independent finite bumps leave a cubic reconciliation difference \(\Delta_{\mathrm{KR}}=\sum_j\mathrm{KRDV01}_j-\mathrm{DV01}_{\parallel}\), derived in \cref{sec:app-reconciliation}. Exact finite-bump equality is therefore unnecessary.
