\chapter{Mathematical and numerical verification}\label{ch:verification}
The independent internal report, \path{paper/verification_report.tex}, supplies separately constructed cash flows, 90-digit exponential references and analytical identities. Proofs establish results under assumptions; tests provide finite numerical evidence.

Par and clean/dirty identities agree to rounding precision. Central repricing agrees independently; its YTM DV01 exceeds the duration approximation by \(1.93\times10^{-9}\) currency units. \Cref{tab:derivatives,tab:convergence} show derivative agreement and initial second-order convergence, followed by cancellation as steps shrink.
