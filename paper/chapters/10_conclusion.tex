\chapter{Conclusion}\label{ch:conclusion}
The four research questions receive conditional answers. Explicit coupon and compounding conventions yield coherent prices and analytical sensitivities. Stable NSS loadings and deterministic calibration represent a smooth curve, without ensuring unique parameters. Zero discounting and interpolated shocks give consistent curve exposures, with first-order key-rate additivity. Independent identities and numerical comparisons support the implementation at tested inputs. The resulting framework demonstrates valuation, term-structure representation and deterministic risk under stated assumptions, without establishing empirical or production validity.
