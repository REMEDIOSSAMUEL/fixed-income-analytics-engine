\chapter{Fixed-rate bond analytics}\label{ch:bonds}
\section{Cash flows and valuation}\label{sec:bond-assumptions}
Assume fixed nonnegative coupons, positive redemption and annual or semiannual payments. Dates are maturity minus multiples of 12 or 6 months, clipping nonexistent days. Settlement-date payments are excluded.

Let \(F>0\) be face value in currency, \(c\geq0\) the annual decimal coupon rate, \(m\in\{1,2\}\) payments per year, and \(\yytm\) nominal annual decimal YTM compounded \(m\) times. The dimensionless accumulation factor is \(B=1+\yytm/m>0\).
\label{sec:bond-accrual}
For settlement \(d_s\), preceding/on-settlement coupon \(d_-\), next coupon \(d_+\), and calendar-day count \(\operatorname{days}\), define accrued-period fraction \(\alpha\), currency coupon \(C\), and accrued interest \(AI\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:accrual}
C=\frac{Fc}{m},\quad
\alpha=\frac{\operatorname{days}(d_s-d_-)}{\operatorname{days}(d_+-d_-)},\quad AI=C\alpha.
\end{equation}
\label{sec:ytm-price}
Index the \(N\) future payments by \(i=1,\ldots,N\), with dates \(d_i\). Their coupon-period distances \(q_i=i-\alpha\) are fractional between coupon dates. Cash flow \(CF_i\) equals \(C\), plus \(F\) at redemption; \(PV_i\) denotes its currency present value. Dirty and clean currency prices \(P_{\mathrm{dirty}},P_{\mathrm{clean}}\) follow exactly from fractional compounding:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ytm-price}
P_{\mathrm{dirty}}=\sum_{i=1}^N\underbrace{CF_iB^{-q_i}}_{PV_i},
\qquad P_{\mathrm{clean}}=P_{\mathrm{dirty}}-AI.
\end{equation}
\label{sec:par}
Par means price equals face; premium and discount mean above and below it. At coupon-date settlement, \(c=\yytm\) implies \(P_{\mathrm{dirty}}=P_{\mathrm{clean}}=F\) (\cref{sec:app-par}); between coupons exact par is not guaranteed.

\section{Duration, convexity and DV01}\label{sec:duration}
Duration measures local rate sensitivity; Macaulay duration \(D_{\mathrm{Mac}}\) is also weighted payment time \parencite{macaulay1938}. Define coupon-based years \(T_i=q_i/m\) and abbreviate dirty price by \(P\); primes denote YTM derivatives. Differentiation yields modified duration \(D_{\mathrm{Mod}}\):
\begin{align}
D_{\mathrm{Mac}}&=\frac{\sum_i T_iPV_i}{P},&
P'&=-\sum_i CF_i\frac{q_i}{m}B^{-q_i-1},\label{eq:price-first}\\
D_{\mathrm{Mod}}&=-\frac{P'}P=\frac{D_{\mathrm{Mac}}}{B}.\label{eq:duration}
\end{align}
Both durations are conventionally expressed in years \parencite{tuckman2011}. For a small decimal yield change \(\delta y\), relative price change is approximately \(-D_{\mathrm{Mod}}\delta y\).

\label{sec:convexity}
For larger yield changes, the approximation also uses price curvature. Normalised convexity \(\mathcal C\), conventionally expressed in years squared \parencite{tuckman2011}, follows from
\begin{equation}\label{eq:convexity}
P''=\sum_i CF_i\frac{q_i(q_i+1)}{m^2}B^{-q_i-2},\qquad \mathcal C=P''/P.
\end{equation}
Positive redemption makes \(\mathcal C>0\). Taylor's local polynomial approximation gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:taylor-price}
\frac{P(\yytm+\delta y)-P(\yytm)}{P(\yytm)}
\approx-D_{\mathrm{Mod}}\delta y+\tfrac12\mathcal C(\delta y)^2.
\end{equation}
\label{sec:ytm-dv01}
DV01 measures the currency price response to one basis point \parencite{tuckman2011}, here using down-minus-up central repricing with decimal bump \(h=10^{-4}\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ytm-dv01}
\mathrm{DV01}_{\mathrm{YTM}}=\frac{P(\yytm-h)-P(\yytm+h)}2
=hPD_{\mathrm{Mod}}+O(h^3).
\end{equation}
The remainder is bounded locally by a constant times \(h^3\). Central repricing retains odd-order nonlinear terms beyond duration; the convexity term cancels between the symmetric prices. Both bumped yields must remain valid. \Cref{sec:app-derivatives,sec:app-dv01} provide the derivations.
