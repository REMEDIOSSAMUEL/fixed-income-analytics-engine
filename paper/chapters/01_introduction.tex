\section{Research aim}\label{sec:aim}
This dissertation examines the development and mathematical verification of a
transparent computational framework for conventional fixed-rate bond valuation,
Nelson--Siegel--Svensson yield-curve modelling, and deterministic interest-rate
risk analysis under explicit financial conventions.
% The implementation already exists. Development refers to the study's
% methodological construction, not an instruction to extend the software.
\section{Research questions}\label{sec:questions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item\label{rq:bond} How can fixed-rate bond prices and yield sensitivities be
derived and implemented transparently under explicit coupon, accrual and
nominal-yield conventions?
\item\label{rq:nss} How does the Nelson--Siegel--Svensson specification represent
a smooth term structure, and what numerical and identification issues arise
in its calibration?
\item\label{rq:risk} How can parallel, shaped and key-rate shocks be represented
consistently for fixed cash flows discounted using an explicitly defined zero curve?
\item\label{rq:verification} To what extent do independent analytical identities,
numerical comparisons and deterministic tests support the correctness of the
implemented calculations under the stated assumptions?
\end{enumerate}
