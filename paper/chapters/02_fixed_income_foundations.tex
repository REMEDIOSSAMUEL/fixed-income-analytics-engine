\subsection{Decimals, percentages and basis points}\label{sec:units}
A basis point (bp) is one hundredth of a percentage point:
\(1\,\bp=0.0001\) in decimal-rate units. Thus \(0.05=5\%\), and a
\(25\,\bp\) increase is a decimal change of \(0.0025\).
