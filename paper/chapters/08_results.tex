\chapter{Illustrative numerical results}\label{ch:results}
Fresh engine calculations use the specifications in \cref{tab:bond-inputs} and illustrative term-structure observations from \path{examples/illustrative_curve.csv}. \Cref{tab:bond-results} identifies a discount bond because its clean price is below face value; its coupon is also below YTM. Modified duration implies approximately \(0.423\%\) dirty-price loss for a 10 bp rise; positive convexity moderates that loss.
