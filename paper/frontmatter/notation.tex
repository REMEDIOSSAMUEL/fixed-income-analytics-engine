\chapter*{Notation and abbreviations}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation and abbreviations}
\label{sec:notation}
The notation distinguishes nominal yield-to-maturity valuation from explicit
zero-curve discounting. Rates are decimals unless a percentage or basis-point
display is explicitly stated. Currency amounts refer to the supplied face
value. \Cref{tab:notation,tab:abbreviations,tab:glossary} collect symbols, abbreviations and terminology; chapter definitions introduce their financial meaning.
