Bond & A debt contract specifying payments from an issuer to its holder. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Fixed-rate bond & A bond whose contractual coupon rate is fixed; this study also holds promised cash flows fixed. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Face or par value & The contractual reference amount used to calculate coupons and, here, repaid at maturity. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Principal & The contractual capital amount, distinct from interest payments. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Redemption payment & The repayment of principal at the bond's maturity; here it equals face value. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Coupon & A contractual interest payment in currency; it is distinct from the coupon rate. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Coupon rate & The annual contractual interest amount divided by face value, expressed internally as a decimal. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Coupon frequency & The number of scheduled coupon payments per year; one or two in this study. & \ref{sec:contract}\\
Settlement date & The valuation and transfer date from which entitlement to future payments is determined under the stated convention. & \ref{sec:dates}\\
Maturity date & The contractual date of final principal repayment. & \ref{sec:dates}\\
Regular coupon schedule & A sequence of payments at the prescribed frequency without irregular first or final periods. & \ref{sec:dates}\\
Accrued interest & The coupon amount attributed to the elapsed part of a coupon period under a specified accrual rule. & \ref{sec:price-quotation}\\
Dirty price & The value including accrued interest; here the discounted value of the strictly future cash flows. & \ref{sec:price-quotation}\\
Clean price & The dirty price less accrued interest under the selected quotation convention. & \ref{sec:price-quotation}\\
Yield to maturity & The single discount yield that equates promised cash-flow present values to dirty price under specified timing and compounding. & \ref{sec:ytm-concept}\\
Nominal compounding & Quotation of an annual rate whose periodic rate is the annual rate divided by compounding frequency; fractional periods require an explicit extension. & \ref{sec:compounding}\\
Continuous compounding & The convention in which accumulation at a constant annual rate over a given horizon is exponential in rate times time. & \ref{sec:compounding}\\
Discount factor & The present value of one unit payable at a specified future time under a stated discounting rule. & \ref{sec:present-value}\\
Present value & The value at the valuation date assigned to a future payment by discounting it. & \ref{sec:present-value}\\
Time value of money & The dependence of the comparison between present and future amounts on their payment dates and the accumulation rule. & \ref{sec:present-value}\\
Basis point & One hundredth of one percentage point: a decimal-rate change of 0.0001. & \ref{sec:units}\\
Percentage point & An absolute difference between percentage quotations; one percentage point is 0.01 in decimal rates. & \ref{sec:units}\\
Par, premium and discount & Par means price equal to face; premium and discount mean above and below face, with the price basis stated. & \ref{sec:par}\\
Duration & A family of measures relating payment timing or local price response to a specified interest-rate variable. & \ref{sec:duration}\\
Macaulay duration & The present-value-weighted mean time to payment, using the stated YTM discounting and time convention. & \ref{sec:duration}\\
Modified duration & The negative first price derivative divided by dirty price, with respect to annual decimal YTM. & \ref{sec:duration}\\
Convexity & The second derivative of dirty price divided by that price, with respect to annual decimal YTM. & \ref{sec:convexity}\\
DV01 & A currency-valued sensitivity for a one-basis-point move; this project defines it by half the down-minus-up repriced difference. & \ref{sec:ytm-dv01}\\
Taylor approximation & A local polynomial representation built from a function's derivatives; omitted terms determine truncation error. & \ref{sec:ytm-dv01}\\
Term structure of interest rates & The dependence of interest rates on maturity at a fixed valuation date under common rate and instrument conventions. & \ref{sec:term-structure}\\
Yield curve & A representation of rates against maturity; its interpretation requires stating which kind of rate is plotted. & \ref{sec:term-structure}\\
Zero-coupon bond & A bond making no interim coupon payments and a single redemption payment. & \ref{sec:zero-par}\\
Spot rate & A rate applicable from valuation to one maturity for a single payment, under a specified compounding convention. & \ref{sec:zero-par}\\
Zero-coupon rate & The single-horizon discount rate associated with a zero-coupon payment; used synonymously with spot rate here. & \ref{sec:zero-par}\\
Par yield & The annual coupon rate that makes a specified bond worth face value under given discount factors, conventionally at coupon-date settlement without accrual. & \ref{sec:zero-par}\\
Parameter & A numerical coefficient or scale specifying a member of a mathematical model family. & \ref{sec:nss-background}\\
Calibration & Selection of model parameters to match supplied observations according to an explicit objective. & \ref{sec:calibration}\\
Residual & The difference between an observed and fitted value; the sign convention here is observed minus fitted. & \ref{sec:calibration}\\
Least squares & A fitting criterion that minimises the sum of squared residuals. & \ref{sec:calibration}\\
Nonlinear optimisation & Numerical search for parameters minimising an objective that is nonlinear in the relevant parameters. & \ref{sec:calibration}\\
Local minimum & A feasible point whose objective is no larger than at sufficiently nearby feasible points; it need not be globally best. & \ref{sec:calibration}\\
RMSE & The square root of the average squared residual; its units match the fitted variable. & \ref{sec:calibration}\\
Nelson--Siegel model & A parametric curve with level, slope-related and curvature loadings governed by a positive decay scale. & \ref{sec:nss-background}\\
Svensson extension & The addition of a second curvature loading with its own coefficient and decay scale to Nelson--Siegel. & \ref{sec:nss-background}\\
Factor loading & A function of maturity multiplying a model coefficient; a loading need not be a directly observed economic variable. & \ref{sec:nss-equations}\\
Asymptotic limit & A value approached by a function as its argument tends to a boundary such as zero or infinity. & \ref{sec:nss-limits}\\
Identification & The ability of observations and model restrictions to distinguish parameter values; weak identification means different values can fit very similarly. & \ref{sec:identification}\\
Bootstrap & Sequential inference of discount factors from instrument prices and cash flows under stated conventions; not implemented here. & \ref{sec:zero-par}\\
Parallel shift & The same additive rate change at every maturity. & \ref{sec:parallel}\\
Full repricing & Recomputing all discounted cash-flow values under a shocked rate or curve, instead of using a local linear approximation. & \ref{sec:parallel}\\
Scenario P\&L & The shocked price minus base price, holding settlement and cash flows fixed; it is not a realised investment return. & \ref{sec:parallel}\\
Steepener & A specified curve change increasing the long-minus-short rate difference; this study states the anchor shifts explicitly. & \ref{sec:shapes}\\
Flattener & A specified curve change decreasing the long-minus-short rate difference; it need not make the whole curve flat. & \ref{sec:shapes}\\
Piecewise-linear interpolation & Joining neighbouring prescribed values by straight-line segments; this study holds endpoint values constant outside the anchor range. & \ref{sec:shapes}\\
Key rate & A rate at a selected maturity used to parameterise a local perturbation of the curve. & \ref{sec:keys}\\
Key-rate basis & A set of maturity-dependent weights specifying how a perturbation at each chosen key affects the curve. & \ref{sec:keys}\\
Partition of unity & A family of weights whose sum is one at every maturity in the specified domain. & \ref{sec:keys}\\
Key-rate DV01 & A currency sensitivity obtained by central full repricing under plus and minus one basis point times one key-rate basis. & \ref{sec:key-dv01}\\
Finite difference & An approximation to a derivative constructed from function values at distinct nearby arguments. & \ref{sec:test-method}\\
Central difference & A finite difference using symmetric positive and negative perturbations around the evaluation point. & \ref{sec:test-method}\\
Truncation error & The error due to omitting terms in an approximation, distinct from floating-point rounding. & \ref{sec:test-method}\\
Numerical tolerance & A specified threshold for accepting a comparison or stopping an algorithm; it must be interpreted in its own units and context. & \ref{sec:tolerances}\\
Floating-point rounding & Representing a numerical result by a nearby value from the finite set available to the machine. & \ref{sec:nss-stability}\\
Cancellation & Loss of relative accuracy when nearly equal rounded quantities are subtracted. & \ref{sec:nss-stability}\\
Conditioning & Sensitivity of the exact solution of a mathematical problem to small input perturbations. & \ref{sec:arithmetic}\\
Numerical stability & The extent to which an algorithm controls the effects of rounding and related computational perturbations. & \ref{sec:nss-stability}\\
Deterministic test & A check with fixed inputs and defined expected properties, designed to be repeatable in a specified environment. & \ref{sec:test-method}\\
Actual/365 timing & Actual elapsed calendar days, including leap days, divided by 365; the denominator does not change in leap years. & \ref{sec:zero-price}\\
Model risk & The risk of misleading conclusions caused by an unsuitable model, interpretation or set of assumptions. & \ref{ch:limitations}\\
Tenor & A stated time horizon or maturity, measured in years here. & \ref{sec:term-structure}\\
